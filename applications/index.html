Electronic Enclosures & PCB Housings

From compact sensor housings to multi-board IoT gateways. We incorporate snap-fit lids, internal PCB standoffs, light pipes, and M2-M4 brass threaded heat-set inserts for secure repeat servicing.

Key Technical Features

  • Custom connector cutouts (USB-C, RJ45, DB9, Terminal blocks)
  • Brass heat-set insert compatibility for long-term thread durability
  • Internal mounting bosses sized for self-tapping or M2.5/M3 screws
  • Ventilation louvers and IP-rated gasket groove geometry

Robotic Mechanisms & Drone Frames

Robotics projects demand stiffness without unnecessary weight. We produce motor mountings, servo horns, gripper fingers, camera gimbals, and drone arm assemblies optimized for strength-to-weight ratios.

Key Technical Features

  • Lightweight infill geometries (Gyroid, 3D Honeycomb) for directional rigidity
  • Vibration-absorbing TPU motor pads and bumper feet
  • High stiffness PA-CF arms with high heat and chemical resistance
