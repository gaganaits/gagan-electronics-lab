Technical Specifications

Threaded Inserts Supported
M2, M2.5, M3, M4, M5, M6, M8 brass ultrasonic/thermal inserts
Surface Treatments
Mechanical sanding (120 to 1000 grit), bead blasting, chemical vapor smoothing
Resin Post-Curing
Wash in high-purity IPA followed by calibrated 405nm UV turntable cure
Hardware Installation
Screws, bearings, magnets, and captive nuts press-fit into CAD cavities

Supported Filaments & Materials

All thermoplastics and resins
