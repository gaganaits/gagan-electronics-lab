Material Selection

Engineering materials for real-world stress.

We stock verified filaments and resins suited for mechanical impact, high heat deflection, chemical contact, and precision fitment.

Complete material property matrix
ThermoplasticHDT: 55°C

PLA+ (High-Impact Polylactic Acid)

Our default material for standard prototyping. Offers minimal warping, sharp corner definition, and smooth surface aesthetics.

Tensile Strength:45 – 55 MPa
Impact Resistance:Moderate
Flexibility:Low (Rigid)
Ideal applications

Dimensionally accurate prototypes, display models, fit-check fixtures, non-heated electronic cases.

ThermoplasticHDT: 75°C

PETG (Polyethylene Terephthalate Glycol)

Combines the ease of printing with UV resistance, moisture resistance, and higher ductility than PLA.

Tensile Strength:50 – 58 MPa
Impact Resistance:High
Flexibility:Moderate (Ductile)
Ideal applications

Outdoor brackets, water-resistant enclosures, mechanical clips, chemical-exposed containers.

ThermoplasticHDT: 95°C

ABS / ASA (Engineering Grade)

Printed inside our sealed heated chamber to prevent layer separation. ASA provides outstanding weather and UV stability.

Tensile Strength:40 – 48 MPa
Impact Resistance:High
Flexibility:Moderate
Ideal applications

Automotive engine-bay adjacent parts, outdoor signage, components requiring acetone smoothing or high heat resistance.

ThermoplasticHDT: 60°C

TPU 95A (Thermoplastic Polyurethane)

Wear-resistant rubber-like polymer capable of absorbing repeated shock and impact without permanent deformation.

Tensile Strength:30 – 35 MPa
Impact Resistance:Very High (Vibration Dampening)
Flexibility:High (Elastomeric, Shore 95A)
Ideal applications

Gaskets, cable grommets, drone bumper guards, vibration dampening feet, flexible protective sleeves.

ResinHDT: 65°C

Engineering Tough Resin (MSLA)

Cured with 405nm UV light. Gives isotropic mechanical strength with no visible layer ridges across all axes.

Tensile Strength:55 – 65 MPa
Impact Resistance:High
Flexibility:Moderate (Elongation at break ~15%)
Ideal applications

Ultra-high precision snap-fits, micro-fluidic channels, wearable device enclosures, fine detail masters.

CompositeHDT: 190°C (at 0.45 MPa)

PA12-CF (Nylon Carbon Fiber Composite)

Continuous chopped carbon fiber embedded in polyamide matrix. Delivers metal-replacement stiffness and exceptional heat resistance.

Tensile Strength:110 – 140 MPa
Impact Resistance:High
Flexibility:Extremely Rigid (High Modulus)
Ideal applications

High-stress drone arms, replacement automotive linkages, CNC machine fixtures, load-bearing robotics.
